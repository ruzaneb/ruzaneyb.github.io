Image Assignment

This is the submission for my image assignment. I used Adobe Photoshop to combine a photo of me fishing with a colorful sunset. I used Generative Fill to blend the water, remove the original reflection, and add details like a fish and distant boats. I also added an image of birds I found on Google and a reflection generated with ChatGPT. Finally, I adjusted the colors and lighting in Pixlr to make the image look more natural.
